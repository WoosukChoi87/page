Artificial Intelligence × Sustainability
We are looking for self-motivated postdocs and passionate students to join our team.

Illuminating a sustainable future through intelligent systems.

AI Science Lab stands at the intersection of artificial intelligence and environmental science. We turn vast atmospheric, climate, and energy data into foresight — building intelligent systems that help the planet not just predict change, but withstand and thrive through it.
